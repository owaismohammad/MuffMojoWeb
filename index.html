It's honestly three steps

How it works

see you soon|

1. Type like normal

Open WhatsApp Web or Instagram DMs and just write your message.

🎉see you soon party|

2. Watch for the bubble

If the last word you typed has an emoji, it shows up right above the cursor.

↑see you soon 🎉|

3. Hit the up arrow

The word becomes the emoji. Didn't want it? Just keep typing and ignore it.

The person behind it

Hey, I'm Owais

I study Electronics & Communication Engineering. I kept losing my train of thought every time I opened the emoji panel to find one specific face, so I wrote a tiny script that does it from the keyboard. That script turned into Muff-Mojo.

It's a side project, so it isn't perfect. If something breaks or there's a word you want added, tell me.

Does it cost anything?

No. It's free on the Chrome Web Store.

Does it send my messages anywhere?

No. The word list lives inside the extension and everything happens in your browser tab.

The bubble isn't showing up

Click inside the message box once and start typing again. Also, words have to match exactly, so it's thumbs_up, not thumbsup.
